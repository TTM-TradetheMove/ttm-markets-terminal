The top box is the session that most recently went live — when New York's core session starts while London is still open, it takes the top spot and reads "Overlap with London"; once London closes it becomes "Core — solo, no overlap". Before that, New York shows "Pre-market" and London stays on top. After the 16:00 ET close it shows "Post-market". Each box lists its exchanges with their open hours and lunch breaks, and the overlap box counts times when exchanges from two different regions are open at once. Cash markets run Monday–Friday, so everything reads Closed over the weekend and counts down to the next open.
