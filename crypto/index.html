Leverage impact

Key risk notes

  • Leverage increases both potential gains and losses, and liquidation can happen before your stop-loss is reached if the stop is too wide for the leverage.
  • Actual fills can differ from planned prices because of volatility, slippage and funding or fees.
  • Break-even win rate is the share of trades you must win, at this R:R, just to cover losses (before fees on a series of trades).
  • This tool gives estimates for education and planning, not financial advice or a trade signal.

Forecast vs actual

By account

Open trades at live prices

How to read it: Actual now = realised balance plus the open P&L of your open trades at the live price (targets already passed are counted as filled, a stop already passed as taken). Forecast = realised balance plus the planned profit if every target of every open trade is hit. Worst = every stop hit. Expected weights those two by that account's own win rate once it has at least 5 closed trades. These are projections from your own plans, not predictions of the market, and fills on your exchange will differ. Not financial advice.

Volume Heatmap

Where trading volume is surging or drying up

Relative volume compares the latest candle's volume with the average of the previous 20 candles on the same timeframe, so 3.0× means three times normal. Zones: Surging (3×+), High (1.5–3×), Normal (0.7–1.5×), Low (0.4–0.7×) and Dry (below 0.4×). Green tiles with ▲ are positive volume (the latest candle closed higher) and red tiles with ▼ are negative volume (it closed lower); the darker the colour, the heavier the volume. Hover a tile for the candle's move and the coin's 24h USDT volume. Filter by type, search a coin, or switch to the table to compare every timeframe.

Pain Zones

Where holders hurt, for any coin

Works on any token, with or without options. It builds a volume-at-price map from real candles to find where most coins changed hands (the average holder's cost), then adds support and resistance and RSI. Price far below the average cost means most recent buyers are underwater. That is where bounces fail at resistance, or where capitulation lows form.

Pick a token and press Analyse.

Levels come from recent exchange candles (Bybit, then OKX, then Binance), so they update every time you analyse. Cost levels are an estimate from volume, not real wallet data. Education only, not financial advice.

Eclipse

Eclipse cycles: Bitcoin and the top 20 tokens

Bitcoin's market cycles have lined up with a repeating run of eclipses. Four lunar eclipses make a red Lunar cycle. The last one starts the green Solar cycle, which runs through three solar eclipses until the next total lunar eclipse starts a new red band.

Max Pain

Where options expiry pulls price

Max pain is the price where the most options expire worthless, so option buyers lose the most and sellers keep the most. Price often drifts toward it as expiry nears, strongest in the final days. Pick a token and an expiry to see where the open interest sits, then check it against RSI on the timeframe you trade.

Loading options data…

Data: Deribit public options open interest, refreshed about every 30 minutes. Only tokens with listed options appear (mainly BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP and a few large altcoins). Max pain is a tendency, not a guarantee, and large expiries or news can override it. This is education, not financial advice.
