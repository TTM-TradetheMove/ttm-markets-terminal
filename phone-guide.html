Trade The Move
Phone setup

Get the TTM Markets Terminal on your phone home screen, sign in and receive the Pro spot pop-ups.

  1. Open the terminal in Safari

    Go to the link below. It must be Safari, not a browser inside Telegram.ttm-tradethemove.github.io/ttm-markets-terminal

  2. Add to Home Screen

    Tap the Share button at the bottom, scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen. The TTM logo is the icon. Tap Add.

  3. Open TTM from the icon

    Always open it from the new TTM icon on your Home Screen, not from Safari.

  4. Sign in: pick your path

    New to TTM: tap Account top right, enter your email, Telegram @name and a password (8 or more characters), then tap Create account. Next time tap Sign in.

    Already have an account (no password yet): open Safari (not the TTM icon), go to the terminal link, tap Account, enter your email and tap Email me a sign-in link instead. Tap the link in the email, then open your Account card and set a password in the Password row. Now open the TTM icon and tap Sign in with your email and new password.

  5. Turn on phone alerts (Pro)

    Tap your name top right, then Phone alerts, Turn on, and tap Allow. TTM spot moves now appear on your lock screen, even with the terminal closed.

Good to know
Alerts cover a spot position opening, a limit order filling and a part TP hitting. On iPhone you need iOS 16.4 or later.
Use your password in the Home Screen app. Email sign-in links open in Safari, not in the app.
If alerts are blocked, open your phone Settings, find TTM under Notifications and switch them on.
Free members get the same spot posts in the Telegram group 14 days later.
