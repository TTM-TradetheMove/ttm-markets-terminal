TTM LENS app

Put TTM LENS on your phone like an app: full screen, its own icon, with Chart, Watchlist, Portfolio, Orders and Alerts along the bottom.

  1. Open TTM LENS in Safari

    Go to the link below. It must be Safari, not a browser inside Telegram.https://ttm-tradethemove.github.io/ttm-markets-terminal/charts/index.html

  2. Sign in

    Sign in with your TTM account. TTM LENS is for Team members while it is in testing.

  3. Add to Home Screen

    Tap the Share button at the bottom, scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen. Name it TTM LENS and tap Add.

  4. Open it from the icon

    Always open TTM LENS from the new icon on your Home Screen. It opens full screen with no browser bars.

  5. Turn on phone alerts

    In the main TTM terminal tap your name, then Phone alerts, Turn on, and tap Allow. Then in TTM LENS tap Alerts to pick your chart alerts.

  1. Open TTM LENS in Chrome

    Go to the link below.https://ttm-tradethemove.github.io/ttm-markets-terminal/charts/index.html

  2. Sign in

    Sign in with your TTM account. TTM LENS is for Team members while it is in testing.

  3. Install the app

    Tap the three dots at the top right, then Install app (or Add to Home screen), then Install.

  4. Open it from the icon

    Open TTM LENS from the new icon. It opens full screen.

  5. Turn on phone alerts

    In the main TTM terminal tap your name, then Phone alerts, Turn on, and tap Allow. Then in TTM LENS tap Alerts to pick your chart alerts.

Good to know
Chart alerts (indicators and drawings) fire while TTM LENS is open. Keep it open on the phone, or pin it in a browser tab on your computer. On iPhone you need iOS 16.4 or later. Watchlists and drawings are saved on each device.
