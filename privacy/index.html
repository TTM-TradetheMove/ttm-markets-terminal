Privacy Statement

Trade The Move (“TTM”, “we”, “us”) · Markets Terminal at ttm.markets · Last updated: 28 November 2026
The short version: we keep the minimum we need to run your account and the terminal. We do not sell your data. We do not store your payment card (we do not take cards) or your passwords. You can delete your account and saved data yourself at any time.

1. Who we are and how to reach us

TTM runs the TTM Markets Terminal (crypto, TradFi, TTM LENS charts, TTM Pills and the community rooms). For any privacy question, or to ask for access to or deletion of your data, use the Message TTM button in the terminal, or message @tradethemove on X.

3. Your choices on tracking

  • We honour your browser’s Do Not Track setting.
  • You can turn usage tracking off for your device in the Account panel (“Turn off”). When off, no usage events are sent.
  • You can turn push notifications off at any time in your browser or phone settings.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal information. We use these service providers to run TTM, and they process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase: our database and sign-in provider (accounts, saved data, chat, usage events).
  • Cloudflare: hosts the website and delivers it to you.
  • GitHub: stores the site’s code and public market data files. No member data is kept there.
  • Market data sources (for example Yahoo Finance, exchange and price APIs): we request public prices from these. They do not receive your account details.
  • Blockchain explorers: we read public USDT transactions to confirm payments.
  • Fourthwall: runs the TTM Shop. If you click through to the shop, their own privacy policy applies.
  • Links to other sites (X, the shop and others) follow those sites’ own policies.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect our members and the service.

5. Where your data is kept

Our providers may store and process data in countries outside Australia, including the United States and other regions where they operate. We choose providers with recognised security practices.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account, plan and saved data: until you delete your account.
  • Payment records: kept as long as needed for our records and any legal requirements.
  • Usage events: kept in anonymised form for analysis. When you delete your account, events are detached from you.

7. Deleting your account and your rights

You can delete your account yourself from the Account panel (“Delete my account”). This permanently removes your profile and your cloud-saved data, and your usage events are detached from you. Anything stored only in your own browser stays on your device until you clear it. You can also ask us to give you a copy of the personal information we hold about you, or to correct it, using the contact in section 1. If you are in Australia you may complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au) if you believe we have mishandled your information.

8. Security

Data is stored with access rules so members can only reach their own information, and connections to the terminal are encrypted. No online service is perfectly secure, so please keep your sign-in details private and tell us straight away if you think your account has been accessed.

9. Children

TTM is for adults. It is not directed at anyone under 18 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

10. Not financial advice

This statement is about privacy. All content in TTM is educational only and is not financial advice. See the TTM Disclaimer shown when you sign in.

11. Changes

If we change how we handle personal information we will update this page and its date, and tell members in the terminal for significant changes.
