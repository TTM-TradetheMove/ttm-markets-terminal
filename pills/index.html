How TTM Pills works

The whole crypto market on one screen. Free, no sign-up.

Reading the chart

  • Colour: green is up, red is down. The stronger the colour, the bigger the move.
  • Size: the bigger the price move, the bigger the pill. Flat coins are small.
  • Position: the biggest gainers fill the top rows. The biggest losers are at the bottom, with the biggest loser at the bottom right.
  • Live: the green LIVE tag at the bottom means prices are streaming in as they happen.

Controls

  • 5M to 1M: pick the time window for the move.
  • Top 125 / Next 125 / All: the biggest coins by market cap, the next group, or everything.
  • Sector and Find a coin: narrow the chart to one theme or one token.
  • Size: switch to market cap or 24h volume if you prefer.

Click any pill

  • See the price, every time window, market cap and volume.
  • Save adds it to your Watchlist. Hide removes it from your chart (get it back under Hidden).
  • CoinGecko opens the coin's data page. TTM LENS opens the full chart for TTM members.
  • Your saved and hidden coins stay on this device.

Use it like an app

  • iPhone / iPad (Safari): tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android (Chrome): tap the menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.
  • Computer (Chrome or Edge): click the install icon at the right of the address bar.
